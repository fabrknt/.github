# FABRKNT

**Sandboxes for crypto market infrastructure.**

Fabrknt builds products for exploring how modern on-chain market systems behave before they are built for real.

Current focus areas:

- **Perp exchange sandboxes**
- **Prime broker sandboxes**
- **Shared risk and margin system design**
- **EVM and Solana market infrastructure**

---

## Sandboxes

| Product | What It Does |
|---------|-------------|
| **EVM Perp Sandbox** | Explore CLOBs, funding, liquidations, and ADL in an EVM-oriented perp system |
| **Solana Perp Sandbox** | Compare Solana-native perp design, execution flow, and exchange risk behavior |
| **EVM Prime Broker Sandbox** | Simulate unified margin, lending, and cross-product risk on an EVM surface |
| **Solana Prime Broker Sandbox** | Explore shared-risk brokerage design and capital coordination on Solana |

These products are currently presented through [fabrknt.com](https://fabrknt.com) as public-preview landing pages with early access.
